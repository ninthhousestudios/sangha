Open a new CC session in any project. Confirm you see `<SANGHA_CONTEXT>` in the
system reminders and that the model calls `session_register` early. Then check

### 2. Wire skills to claim advisory locks

`/done` and `/reflect` should claim a lock on artifacts they write before
writing. Pattern: claim → write → release.

### 3. Loose ends

- **CLI `sangha locks` (non-JSON) doesn't print branch** — trivial to add
- **Migrations are now idempotent** (`IF NOT EXISTS`) — future migrations must
  maintain this or add versioning
- **Connection lifecycle under reconnect** not yet tested in the wild
- Chitta `After=postgresql.service` doesn't guarantee PG is accepting
  connections — may want `postgresql-online.target` or a retry loop
